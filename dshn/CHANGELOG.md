# Changelog

*升级日志*

## 0.2.2.5.1

- Engine updated to DeepSeek Harness `0.2.2rc5` (needs Node 24).

  *引擎升级到 DeepSeek Harness `0.2.2rc5`（要求 Node 24）。*

- Paste an image straight into the chat box and ask about it (up to six at a time).

  *可以直接往输入框里粘图提问了（一次最多 6 张）。*

- Switch away mid-answer and come back: the finished result comes back immediately instead of
  running again. Conversations also stay in sync across devices.

  *答题中途切走再回来，会直接拿到已经跑完的结果，不用重等一遍；多个设备之间会话保持同步。*

- Smaller image (1.5 GB instead of 2.2 GB) — identical files inside the engine are linked
  instead of copied.

  *镜像更小（2.2 GB → 1.5 GB）：引擎里重复的文件改成硬链接，不再各存一份。*
